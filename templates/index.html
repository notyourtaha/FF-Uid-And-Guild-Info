              FAQ
              
Frequently asked questions

            

          


          

            

              Is DropZone free to use?
              
Yes. The player lookup interface is designed to be accessible without creating an account.

            


            

              Do you store my searches?
              
Recent search history is stored locally in your browser so you can quickly reuse UIDs. It is not needed for the lookup request itself.

            


            

              Why can't I see some player information?
              
Free Fire account responses can vary. If a field is not returned by the service, DropZone cannot reliably display it.

            


            

              Can I use DropZone on mobile?
              
Yes. The interface is responsive and optimized for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
